Before you get in touch

A few useful
answers.

What should I include in my enquiry?

A short description of the job, your suburb, approximate dimensions and your preferred timing are a useful start. Photos of the existing space and examples of the finish you like can help too.

Can I enquire about a smaller job?

Yes, describe the repair or improvement you have in mind. The scope, location and current availability will need to be checked before a job can be confirmed.

Do I need drawings before getting in touch?

You can start with an idea or a few reference photos. Depending on the work, drawings, engineering or approvals may be needed before construction can begin.

Which areas do you service?

The service area is being confirmed for this website preview. Include your suburb in the enquiry so the location can be checked.
